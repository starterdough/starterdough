Starterdough runs in the browser, installs as an app from the browser's menu, and works the same
on desktop and mobile.

## Create your account

Sign up with your email address and a password, then open the link in the confirmation email. You
can also sign in with a passkey or a social account where those are offered.

## Keep your account safe

Under **Settings → Security** you can add a passkey and switch on two-factor authentication, and
under **Settings → Sessions** you can sign out devices you no longer use.

## Build on the API

Everything the app does goes through one HTTP API. The [API reference](/reference/api-reference/)
lists every endpoint, rendered from the live OpenAPI document.
